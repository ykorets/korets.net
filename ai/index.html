Marco — autonomous outbound agent

Infrastructure + signals + research + email/LinkedIn · human-in-the-loop

Marco builds the sending layer first: secondary domains, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, mailboxes, warm-up, placement tests, per-mailbox caps, and reputation monitoring. The same control plane is designed to scale from one mailbox to a thousand. No campaign launches until its senders clear the deliverability gates.

Then it finds the right moment, not just the right job title. Marco searches across commercial databases, LinkedIn, company sites, job boards, industry directories, and public or first-party data. It looks for signals such as leadership changes, hiring, funding, website intent, and new permits. It scores and deduplicates prospects, researches each company, then writes a short message in the sender's voice. Email and LinkedIn sequences run automatically; Marco triages replies every 15 minutes and flags positive intent in Telegram. Factual claims, pricing, and objections stay behind human approval.

A human operator no longer has to choose between depth and volume. Signals decide who and when; research decides what to say; infrastructure makes sure it arrives. Every send and reply becomes data for the next campaign.

Behind the Watt — AI with receipts

Open data + evidence pipeline · public records, LLM extraction, human approval

An open database tracking which behind-the-meter power projects at data centers are actually operating. Watchers monitor permits, filings, and public registries; the pipeline archives each source, uses AI to extract and cross-check claims, then holds publication for human approval. Every fact retains source-level provenance and is exposed through CSV, JSON, OpenAPI, and read-only MCP.

AI handles the volume, but the model is never the source: every published claim stays inspectable.
