Behind the Watt — AI with receipts

Open data + evidence pipeline · public records, LLM extraction, human approval

An open database tracking which behind-the-meter power projects at data centers are actually operating. Watchers monitor permits, filings, and public registries; the pipeline archives each source, uses AI to extract and cross-check claims, then holds publication for human approval. Every fact retains source-level provenance and is exposed through CSV, JSON, OpenAPI, and read-only MCP.

AI handles the volume, but the model is never the source: every published claim stays inspectable.
