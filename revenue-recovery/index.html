The Leaks

Three places your revenue is walking out the door

Every shop knows about the first leak. Almost nobody measures the second — and it's usually the biggest.

of estimates never get a second follow-up

A shop sending 100 estimates a month at a $5K average has ~$200K sitting open at any time. Most sales close after the 4th touch. Most shops stop at one.

85% of those callers won't leave a voicemail — they call your competitor. In HVAC, 62% of calls come in after hours, and emergency calls are worth 2–3x a routine ticket.

The System

Three modules. One connected install.

Each module attacks one leak. They share your CRM as the single source of truth, and every automated action is logged — with a human approval gate where it matters.

Module 1 · The biggest leak first

Estimate Recovery Engine

An AI agent works your open and aged estimates on a disciplined cadence — personalized SMS and email, plus a natural-voice follow-up call that answers questions and captures objections. Every response is logged to the estimate in your CRM; anything warm is handed to your comfort advisor to close.

Shops that systematize follow-up convert 30–40% of previously cold estimates. This is found money: the lead is paid for, the visit is done, the quote is written.
Module 2 · Stop new losses

24/7 AI Front Desk

A voice agent answers every call — after hours, weekends, and overflow during the summer rush. It books jobs directly into your calendar, triages true emergencies for escalation, and texts the caller a confirmation. Answering on the first ring alone lifts booking rates ~15%.

Missed-call revenue loss goes to zero without adding a CSR seat.
Module 3 · Compound the base

Customer Base Machine

Your past-customer database becomes a revenue channel: seasonal reactivation campaigns segmented by equipment age and last visit, maintenance-plan enrollment and renewals, and frictionless review requests at the moment the job closes.

Recurring revenue and organic lead flow that compounds every month — at a fraction of paid-lead cost.
