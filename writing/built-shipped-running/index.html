The operating principle

Persistence and volume belong to software. Authority stays with people.

Every system in the series uses explicit rules, a human gate for consequential actions, and a record that can be audited after the fact.

AI does the persistent work

Monitoring, extraction, comparison, drafting, and follow-up at a volume people cannot sustain.

Humans approve what matters

Money, facts, promises, strategy, and actions that are difficult to reverse.

Everything is logged

Inputs, proposals, tool calls, approvals, final actions, outcomes, and operating cost.
