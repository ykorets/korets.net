01 / Acquisition funding

One practice. Two financing phases.

Compare the platform’s cash contribution with bank financing and a seller note. All purchase-price shares sum to 100%; closing costs are paid by the platform on top.

Bank financing is unavailable for the first 24 months. Bank-funded structures below are illustrations for month 25 onward. For launch acquisitions, use the seller-financed structure. Applying a structure to the investor model preserves the 24-month bank restriction.

Before bank availability, the investor model uses the separate first-phase cash contribution and seller financing for the balance. Applying this structure changes the later-phase financing, firm economics and loan terms; it preserves the $1.5M seed commitment. Investor returns use a separate illustrative participation assumption.

Partner participation is negotiated for each acquisition.

We prefer a continuing operator to retain a meaningful stake in the local business. That can give the seller liquidity now and a share of future growth, while the platform acquires control. The percentage, management role, salary, distributions and eventual buyout are individual deal terms; 80/20 is an illustration, not a standard offer.

Retained equity is different from a seller note: equity participates in future results, while the note is a repayment obligation. The financing tools on this page still assume a full purchase without retained seller equity.

What the seller receives, and when.

The seller receives the cash down payment plus bank-funded consideration at closing, then monthly payments on the seller note. The schedule assumes full repayment on time, no standby, no seller equity and no taxes. Closing costs are not additional proceeds to the seller.

03 / Debt-service coverage

Does the practice cover its debt?

DSCR = annual firm EBITDA / annual acquisition debt payments. The entry and integrated ratios use the selected firm margins, before platform overhead and customer acquisition costs.

A ratio below 1× means firm EBITDA does not cover the modeled debt service. A higher ratio leaves more room for reinvestment; the full investor model additionally deducts platform costs and applies cash reserves.

05 / Negotiation checklist

Purchase and transition

Confirm client retention, revenue quality, collections, seller support and closing costs before relying on the purchase multiple.

Debt terms

Confirm lender eligibility, security, covenants, interest, amortization and any seller-note subordination or payment standby.

Platform liquidity

Review combined debt service and cash reserves across all acquired practices. A financeable individual firm does not alone prove platform solvency.
