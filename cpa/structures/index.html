01 / Acquisition funding

One practice. Three financing approaches.

Compare the platform’s cash contribution with bank financing and a seller note. All purchase-price shares sum to 100%; closing costs are paid by the platform on top.

Run this structure in the investor model ↗

Before bank availability, the investor model uses the separate first-phase cash contribution and seller financing for the balance. Applying this structure changes the later-phase financing, firm economics and loan terms; it keeps the $1M / 50% pre-seed unchanged.

02 / Seller economics

What the seller receives, and when.

The seller receives the cash down payment plus bank-funded consideration at closing, then monthly payments on the seller note. The schedule assumes full repayment on time, no standby, no seller equity and no taxes. Closing costs are not additional proceeds to the seller.

03 / Debt-service coverage

Does the practice cover its debt?

DSCR = annual firm operating profit / annual acquisition debt payments. The entry and integrated ratios use the selected firm margins, before platform overhead and customer acquisition costs.

A ratio below 1× means firm operating profit does not cover the modeled debt service. A higher ratio leaves more room for reinvestment; the full investor model additionally deducts platform costs and applies cash reserves.

04 / Equity and investor ownership

Acquisition debt does not issue new shares.

The base case uses cash, bank debt and a seller note. The pre-seed investor remains at 50% unless the optional later equity round is enabled.

Seller equity would add ownership dilution and a separate valuation negotiation. It is excluded from this version; the former model’s assumed seller pool is not carried into the $1M / 50% starting deal.

05 / Negotiation checklist

Purchase and transition

Confirm client retention, revenue quality, collections, seller support and closing costs before relying on the purchase multiple.

Debt terms

Confirm lender eligibility, security, covenants, interest, amortization and any seller-note subordination or payment standby.

Platform liquidity

Review combined debt service and cash reserves across all acquired practices. A financeable individual firm does not alone prove platform solvency.
