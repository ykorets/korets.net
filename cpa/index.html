Model assumptions

All scenarios begin with the same $1M pre-seed investment for 50% ownership. Adjust assumptions to explore the outcome.

Where the investor’s return comes from

Exit equity = enterprise value − outstanding acquisition debt + remaining cash. The investor receives their ownership share of equity and each distribution.

How to read this model

¹ Net EBITDA is annualized firm operating profit after platform overhead and customer acquisition costs. Margins improve gradually over the integration period. Acquisition debt amortizes monthly; deal closing costs are expensed in cash at acquisition. Purchases require a platform cash reserve. Debt availability is an assumption, not a committed facility.

² Investor cash includes annual distributions and exit proceeds in the final year. IRR uses actual monthly cash-flow timing; MOIC is total receipts divided by the original investment. Additional equity, if enabled, dilutes the pre-seed investor from the month it arrives. The base case has no additional equity and no seller equity pool.

Illustrative, pre-tax scenario model. No preferred return, liquidation preference, transaction taxes, exit fees, working-capital changes or legal ownership restrictions are modeled. A cash shortfall stops the simulation; exit returns are then unavailable. These assumptions are editable projections, not forecasts or guaranteed returns.
