How to read this model

¹ Annualized EBITDA is the month-end platform EBITDA run rate (current month × 12), after platform overhead and customer acquisition costs. Annualized revenue is also a month-end run rate, not revenue earned during the year. EBITDA means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization; it is not net income. Margins improve gradually over the integration period. Later acquisitions use their own entry size, purchase multiple and integrated margin; earlier firms retain their original assumptions. The blended size represents a count-weighted planning average, not a simulated distribution of individual deal sizes. Acquisition debt amortizes monthly; deal closing costs are expensed in cash at acquisition. Purchases require a cash reserve. Debt availability is an assumption, not a committed facility.

Cash balance is the money remaining at period end after funding, acquisitions, expenses, debt payments and distributions. It is a balance, not revenue, EBITDA or net income.

² Investor proceeds includes annual distributions and exit proceeds in the final year. IRR uses actual monthly cash-flow timing; MOIC is total receipts divided by seed capital actually funded. Later equity funding affects the business cash forecast, but its terms are not modeled. When a later round occurs, investor-return metrics are unavailable. The Base assumes a $2.5M growth round in month 25; the historical no-growth-round plan remains available in the overview comparison.

Illustrative, pre-tax scenario model. Capital expenditures, income taxes, depreciation and amortization are not modeled. No preferred return, liquidation preference, transaction taxes, exit fees, working-capital changes or legal ownership restrictions are modeled. A cash shortfall stops the simulation; exit returns are then unavailable. These assumptions are editable projections, not forecasts or guaranteed returns.
