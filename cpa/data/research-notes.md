## Excluded or weakened claims

- Anonymous or composite automation success stories: No identifiable client or reproducible measurement; research leads only.

- Vendor-generated future revenue from saved hours: Capacity multiplied by an assumed billing rate is not realized contribution.

- Generic product extraction accuracy applied to a named customer: Product-wide accuracy is not a customer-specific test set; denominators may differ.

- FTE avoided presented as cash removed: Requires an actual planned hire or expense counterfactual and full service costs.

- JHR/Vinance client doubling and $180K hiring avoidance: Targets in the archived case, not verified achieved outcomes.

- Steve Tonkin 30–50% time savings: Projected rollout upside; the archived early reported result is 20%.

- BDO UserVerse 50% approval-time reduction: An anticipated target; the archived operating time claim is 10–20%.

- MHA UK 45,000 hours: Volume-based forecast, not a measured annual saving.



## Pilot measurement plan



Count verified cost removed or incremental contribution from accepted additional work, subtract added technology/support/review costs, and avoid counting the same freed hour twice. Capacity alone is not cash savings.

- cohort (cases): Record tax year, return/process type and complexity; keep comparable before/after populations. Current value: unavailable.

- touch_time (minutes/case by role): Include preparation, professional review, rework, exceptions and fallback; distinguish sampled from instrumented time. Current value: unavailable.

- cycle_time (elapsed hours/case): Measure from a defined start to reviewer-usable output; separate waiting from staff effort. Current value: unavailable.

- quality (corrections/case and material-error rate): Define denominator and materiality before comparing; include downstream outcomes when observable. Current value: unavailable.

- adoption (percent eligible cases): Count work using the path and why eligible cases bypass it. Current value: unavailable.

- recurring_cost (USD/month and USD/case): Include software, model usage, external support and incremental internal review. Current value: unavailable.

- realized_economic_effect (USD/period): Verified removed cost plus incremental contribution, less incremental delivery costs; no double-counted hours. Current value: unavailable.
