The opportunity

01

Keep the public experience simple.
Make the sales experience exceptional.

The configurator is a strong idea. Its value grows when it appears at the right moment in the buyer journey.

The public website should make Sanctuary easy to understand and compelling to explore. The richer configuration experience can then help a qualified buyer see their own home, make trade-offs, and move forward with confidence.

Strategic extension

04

Digital Sales Experience Blueprint

The pilot proves the technology. The Blueprint connects it to the full buyer journey.

  1. 01

    Inspire

    Tell the story of the location, lifestyle, homes, and the possibilities of personalization.

  2. 02

    Qualify

    Guide the prospect toward the right home, budget range, timeline, and visit preference.

  3. 03

    Configure

    Give the qualified buyer a personal link to explore the relevant home, schemes, and upgrades.

  4. 04

    Guide

    Continue the configuration with a sales advisor remotely, on a large screen, or at Sanctuary.

  5. 05

    Propose

    Turn saved choices into a structured summary, proposal, pricing record, and CRM follow-up.

  6. 06

    Continue

    Extend the same source of truth into selections, approvals, changes, and client communication.

The value

One source of truth for the home, the options, the price, and the buyer’s current decision.

Delivery approach

05

Unreal quality,
delivered for the web.

Unreal remains the visual source. The browser receives a purpose-built version of each experience.

01

Prepare

Audit the project, separate configurable components, simplify complex materials, and define the option matrix.

02

Optimize

Deliver compressed geometry and textures through a CDN, loading only the selected home and likely next options.

03

Experience

Use a lightweight viewer, a separate configuration layer, and progressive loading for smooth interaction.

LayerRecommendationRole
ExteriorThree.js

Real-time rotation, material changes, and modular add-ons.

AssetsGLB + Meshopt + KTX2

Compact geometry and GPU-friendly compressed textures.

InteriorPhoto Sphere Viewer

Tiled panoramas with smooth design-scheme transitions.

ConfigurationJSON / API rules

Options, compatibility, pricing adjustments, and saved state.

Plan

06

A phased engagement with clear decision points.

Discovery, the working Configurator Pilot, and the Digital Sales Experience Blueprint form one focused initial engagement. The full rollout is scoped from evidence, not assumptions.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Audit the Unreal project, select the pilot home, confirm options, and set target devices.

    3–5 business days
  2. 02

    Configurator Pilot

    Build and test the focused exterior and interior configuration experience.

    2–4 weeks
  3. 03

    Experience Blueprint

    Define the full buyer journey, product architecture, integrations, and rollout roadmap.

    1–2 weeks
  4. 04

    Measured Rollout

    Expand across priority homes, sales workflows, showroom use, and the broader catalog.

    Scoped after pilot

Initial commercial structure

One fixed-scope package

Discovery, the working pilot, and the Blueprint. Final investment is confirmed after the asset review.

Rollout structure

Transparent units

Platform implementation, asset production per home, add-on and room, plus agreed pricing, proposal, CRM, or showroom integrations.

Outcome

07

What success looks like

The experience should feel as considered as the community itself: visually refined, simple to understand, and useful at every stage of the decision.

  • A visitor understands the possibilities without learning a complicated interface.
  • A qualified prospect can personalize a relevant home and return to the same configuration.
  • A sales advisor continues the conversation from the prospect’s saved choices.
  • Upgrades are visible, understandable, and connected to indicative investment.
  • The system can expand across the home catalog without rebuilding the experience.
